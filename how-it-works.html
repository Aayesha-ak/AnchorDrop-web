The AnchorDrop Compatibility Formula

To ensure safe navigation and tie-ups, our platform evaluates 3 fundamental physical marine constraints:

1. Length Check (LOA)

Vessel.Length ≤ Dock.MaxLength

Ensures the boat does not overhang dangerously into navigation fairways or block neighboring slips.

2. Beam Check (Width)

Vessel.Beam ≤ Dock.MaxBeam

Ensures catamarans and wide cruisers have adequate clearance between pilings and fenders.

3. Draft Check (Depth)

Vessel.Draft ≤ Dock.MinDepth

Ensures the keel or propeller does not strike the seabed at mean low water (MLW) tide conditions.

* Note: Compatibility is a preliminary dimensional check to aid decision-making and does not substitute local nautical charts or captain navigational responsibility.
